Radio Doom & Gloom

The "Radio Doom & Gloom" is a metaphor from Acceptance and Commitment Therapy. The mind, like a radio, is always broadcasting negative thoughts, worries, self-criticism.

This research took that metaphor as a starting point, but extended it. In the experiment, participants were able to interact with a recording of their own negative thought in real time — hearing it at a different pitch, distorted, echoed, slowed. The interface on this website gives you an impression of that experience through pre-recorded examples.

View the ACT Radio Doom & Gloom metaphor
Real-life setup

In the original experiment, participants recorded their own negative self-referential thought. They then listened to it played back through the interface and explored various audio modulations themselves. Before and after, they rated how much they believed the thought, how much emotional discomfort it caused, and how willing they were to accept it.

Research question

To what extent does interacting with a metaphor-based tangible audio interface facilitate cognitive defusion and reduce the impact of negative self-referential thoughts in participants?
